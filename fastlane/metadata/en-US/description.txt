RC Joystick: WiFi and BLE is a remote-control app for compatible, user-owned RC vehicles. A vehicle with electronics and firmware that support the app's control protocol is required; the app does not control ordinary RC products automatically.

Choose the connection that matches your vehicle:

• Bluetooth Low Energy discovers nearby compatible vehicles, connects to the selected vehicle, and sends control commands directly.
• Wi-Fi joins your vehicle's local network and sends low-latency UDP control commands to its configured address.

Control features:

• A floating driving joystick appears where you touch and controls the left and right motors.
• A separate floating joystick controls the turret while preserving its last target when released.
• Independent driving and turret sensitivity settings provide precise or fast response.
• Optional gyroscope control moves the turret from its current position.
• Laser, fire, and trigger buttons control optional auxiliary outputs implemented by compatible vehicle hardware.
• Connection status and safe-disconnect behavior help stop movement and auxiliary outputs when the connection changes.

The app stores control preferences and Wi-Fi configuration on the device. It does not require an account and contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, in-app purchases, or cloud service.
