# Privacy Policy for RC Joystick: WiFi and BLE

Effective date: August 8, 2026

RC Joystick: WiFi and BLE does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app does not use advertising, analytics, tracking, user accounts, in-app purchases, or cloud services.

## Bluetooth and Local Network Access

Bluetooth access is used to discover and connect to compatible user-owned RC vehicles. For example, moving a joystick sends driving and turret control commands to the vehicle selected by the user.

Local network and Hotspot Configuration access are used only to join a compatible vehicle's Wi-Fi network and exchange control and connection-status messages with that vehicle. These messages remain on the local connection and are not sent to the developer or a third party.

## Information Stored on the Device

Control preferences and Wi-Fi connection settings are stored locally on the user's device. Wi-Fi passwords are stored in the iOS Keychain. The developer does not receive this information.

## Third Parties

The app does not include third-party advertising, analytics, tracking, or data-collection SDKs.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when the app's data practices change. The effective date above identifies the latest version.
